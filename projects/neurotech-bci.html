Neurotech BCI

Data Acquisition & Processing · Sept 2023 - June 2025

About This Project

Python NumPy / pandas MNE BrainFlow OpenBCI

As a Data Acquisition & ML Engineer for Neurotech@Davis for 2 years before eventually becoming one of the Projects Divison Co-Heads, I worked on the data side of two brain-computer interface projects:

(1) A drone control system using SSVEP (steady-state visually evoked potential) signals
(2) A virtual camera control system using cVEP (code-modulated visually evoked potentials).

My role in the team consisted of assisting with designing the experimental data-collection procedures based on rigourous literature review, and helping build the preprocessing pipeline that turned raw EEG signal into usable data. As a proof-of-concept effort, our project's progress halted at data collection and processing and we were unable to progress to a fully deployed system. However, the pipeline, preliminary results, and concept of both projects were presented at the California Neurotechnology Conference in both 2024 and 2025.

Key Contributions

  • Designed experimental data-collection procedures for SSVEP and cVEP signal acquisition, based on literature review
  • Preprocessed 80+ individual EEG trials using NumPy, pandas, MNE, and BrainFlow
  • Presented the data pipeline and preliminary results at the California Neurotechnology Conference in 2024 & 2025
